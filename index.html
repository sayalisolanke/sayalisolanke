Research

2026 — PRESENT · IISER THIRUVANANTHAPURAM

Ligand Synthesis & Main-Group Metal Chemistry

Master’s thesis research in organometallic chemistry focused on the synthesis and development of β-diketiminate (NacNac) ligands and their derivatives.

Focus: Synthesis of different NacNac ligand frameworks and derivatives, followed by their coordination with main-group metal centres to investigate the resulting complexes and their chemical reactivity.

Current work: Designing and synthesising NacNac-based ligands, characterising the ligand systems and metal complexes, and studying their coordination behaviour and reactivity with main group metals.

Area: Organometallic & coordination chemistry.

2025 — 2026 · IISER THIRUVANANTHAPURAM

Seed & Starch Biology

NMR-based characterization of molecular changes in rice seeds associated with PIMT mediated repair and aging.

Focus: Studied starch structure and seed metabolite profiles to investigate molecular changes associated with seed aging and protein repair pathways.

Approach: Used solid-state CP/MAS NMR to examine structural features of starch containing seed samples and solution-state ¹H NMR to characterize metabolite profiles.

Biological system: Compared rice seed samples including wild-type and PIMT1/PIMT2 overexpression lines under control and artificial aging conditions, examining differences in starch and metabolite profiles.

2024 - 2026 · IISER THIRUVANANTHAPURAM

Topochemical Chemistry

Synthesis and characterization of functional organic monomers to investigate their crystallinity and solid-state organization.

Focus: Designed and synthesized functional monomers to study how their molecular structure influences crystal formation and solid-state packing.

Work: Prepared monomers using organic transformations including amide coupling, Boc deprotection, esterification, acylation, and Diels-Alder chemistry.

Characterization: Used NMR, PXRD, DSC, TGA, IR/UV spectroscopy, and MALDI-MS to characterize the synthesized compounds and investigate their structural and solid-state properties.
