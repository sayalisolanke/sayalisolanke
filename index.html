RESEARCH

Research

I make β-diketiminate (NacNac) ligands and study how they bind to main-group and transition metals.

My current work focuses on synthesising the ligands, characterising them, and understanding how NacNac frameworks coordinate.

I used NMR to characterise starch and metabolites in seeds.

I worked with rice seeds using solution-state and solid-state NMR to compare metabolite profiles in control and aged seeds and to study changes in starch structure.

I made and characterised functional monomers for topochemical and Diels–Alder reactions.

The work covered organic synthesis, purification, reaction setup, and characterisation by NMR, PXRD, DSC, TGA, IR, UV and MALDI-MS.
