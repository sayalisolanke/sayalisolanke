03 / RESEARCH

Research experience.

2026 — PRESENT CURRENT PROJECT

NacNac Ligand & Metal Complex Chemistry

Current BS-MS major research project involving the synthesis of NacNac (β-diketiminate) derivative ligands and their use in the synthesis and study of metal complexes.

  • Synthesis of NacNac derivative ligands.
  • Synthesis of metal complexes using different NacNac-derived ligands.
  • Exploring ligand coordination and reactivity in metal-complex chemistry.
AUG 2024 — AUG 2026

Topochemical Polymerization

Research involving the synthesis and characterization of functional monomers for topochemical chemistry.

  • Amide coupling, Boc deprotection, esterification and acylation.
  • Purification using chromatographic techniques.
  • NMR spectroscopy and mass spectrometry.
  • PXRD, DSC and TGA characterization.
  • Studying relationships between molecular structure, crystal packing and polymerization behaviour.
2025 — 2026

Seed & Starch Biology

NMR-based characterization of starch-derived metabolites and molecular changes associated with seed aging.

  • Solid-state CP/MAS NMR spectroscopy.
  • Solution-state ¹H NMR analysis.
  • Rice seed systems including wild-type, OsPIMT1 and OsPIMT2 lines.
  • Comparison of metabolite profiles under control and aging conditions.
  • Sample preparation, spectral acquisition and interpretation.
DEC 2025

Computational Materials Physics

Winter research project involving coursework and basic theoretical analysis of electronic and structural properties of solids.

JUN — JUL 2024

Parasitology

Summer research internship involving the study of parasitic organisms using microscopy-based approaches.

  • Cestodes from sheep.
  • Nematodes from fish.
  • Dissection, staining and microscopy.
  • Basic pathology assessment.
