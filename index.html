Research

Research

I make beta diketiminate (NacNac) ligands and study how they bind to main group and transition metals.

Right now I am working on synthesising the ligands, characterising them, and understanding how the NacNac frameworks coordinate.

I used NMR to characterise starch and metabolites in seeds.

I worked with rice seeds, using solution state and solid state NMR to compare metabolite profiles in control and aged seeds, and to see how the structure of starch changes.

I made and characterised functional monomers for topochemical and Diels Alder reactions.

The work covered organic synthesis, purification, setting up reactions, and characterisation by NMR, PXRD, DSC, TGA, IR, UV and MALDI MS.
